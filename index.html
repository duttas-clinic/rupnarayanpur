100% Robotic Infrastructure

For the past 20 years, we've utilized completely robotic infrastructure with a capacity of 240 tests per hour, ensuring the most reliable and accurate reports in the district.

95% Painless Collection

We use ultra-fine needles and expert phlebotomists. Our patients experience a virtually painless, frictionless service in a 100% private environment.

Nearly 50 Years of Diagnostic Excellence

Established in 1976, Dutta's Clinical Laboratory has been the cornerstone of trusted healthcare in Rupnarayanpur for nearly half a century. As a highly accredited and Government of West Bengal approved diagnostic center.

Our longevity is a testament to the trust placed in us by thousands of families across the district who rely on us for their most critical health decisions.
