Why Choose Us

100% Robotic Infrastructure

For the past 20 years, we've utilized completely robotic infrastructure with a capacity of 240 tests per hour, ensuring the most reliable reports.

95% Painless Collection

We use ultra-fine needles and expert phlebotomists for a virtually painless, frictionless service in a 100% private environment.

Gold Standard of Integrity

We avoid home collection. Blood samples degrade during transit. By visiting us, your sample is processed immediately, guaranteeing 100% accuracy.
